---
Bug report: {BUG}

1. Reproduce it first — do not fix what you cannot reproduce. Show the failing behavior.
2. Isolate the root cause; explain it in two sentences before touching code.
3. Fix it minimally. No drive-by refactors.
4. Prove the fix: re-run the reproduction and the relevant tests.
